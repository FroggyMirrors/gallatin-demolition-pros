Exterior Demolition

Professional Exterior Demolition in Gallatin

Exterior demolition covers anything on the outside of your home or property that needs to be removed: a screened porch, front stoop, awning, small carport, old siding, a gazebo, pergola or playset. It's often the first step in a renovation, or a way to remove something that's become unsafe.

Because many exterior structures are attached to the house, the job is as much about protecting what stays as removing what goes. We plan tie-in points, protect windows and roofing, and leave openings secured so your contractor can pick up right where we stop.

What's Included

  • Porch, stoop and step removal
  • Siding, trim and soffit removal
  • Awning, small carport and canopy removal
  • Gazebos, pergolas and playsets
  • Temporary weatherproofing of openings with plastic or boards
  • Hauling all debris, with metal recycled

What to Expect

How Your Exterior Demolition Project Works

  1. On-site quote

    We look at what's coming off, how it's attached to the house, utilities, roof tie-ins and access. We write down exactly where demolition stops.

  2. Permits and utilities

    Larger exterior demolition may need a City of Gallatin permit, and any electrical, gas or plumbing must be safely disconnected first.

  3. Protection

    Windows, doors, AC units, landscaping and walkways near the work area are covered or protected.

  4. Demolition

    The structure is taken apart in a planned order, and the connection to the house is detached carefully.

  5. Secure and clean

    Any openings are temporarily sealed against weather, debris is hauled away and the area is cleaned up.

How to Prepare

  • Clear furniture and belongings out of the porch or work area
  • Have a contractor lined up to close up or rebuild if the structure is attached
  • Schedule utility disconnects if there's wiring or plumbing
  • Let neighbors know if equipment will be in the driveway

What Affects the Price

Exterior demolition pricing depends on the size and type of structure, how it's attached to the house, roofing and foundation materials, utilities, permit needs and site access. We provide a detailed written quote after seeing it in person.

FAQ

Exterior Demolition FAQ

Will you close up the wall after removing a porch?

We temporarily weatherproof openings with plastic or boards. Permanent repairs such as siding, framing or roofing should be done by your general contractor.

Can you remove vinyl or aluminum siding?

Yes. We remove siding, trim and soffits ahead of new siding installation and recycle the metal.

Do you demolish whole houses, barns or large buildings?

No. We focus on interiors and smaller outdoor features. For a full house, barn or large building demolition, call us and we'll refer you to a trusted contractor who handles that work.
