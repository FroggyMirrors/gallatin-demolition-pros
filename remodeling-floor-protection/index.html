Remodeling Floor Protection

Professional Remodeling Floor Protection in Gallatin

Remodels are hard on floors. Work boots track grit, tools get dropped, ladders scrape, paint and adhesive spill, and heavy materials get dragged across rooms. Repairing or refinishing damaged hardwood or replacing cracked tile after a remodel can cost thousands, while protecting it up front costs a fraction of that.

We install professional-grade floor protection before demolition or construction starts. We serve homeowners, general contractors, painters, flooring installers, cabinet installers and builders throughout the Gallatin area. You can hire us for setup, for removal when the job is finished, or for both.

What's Included

  • Cleaning floors before protection goes down so no grit is trapped underneath
  • Heavy-duty breathable floor protection board for hardwood, tile and LVP
  • Corrugated plastic or hardboard in high-traffic and heavy-equipment areas
  • Adhesive carpet protection film for carpeted paths
  • All seams taped so nothing slips under
  • Stair tread and landing protection
  • Removal and disposal of all protection at the end of the project

What to Expect

How Your Remodeling Floor Protection Project Works

  1. Plan the path

    We walk the home with you or your contractor and map the work areas and traffic paths, from the entry door to the work zone.

  2. Clean first

    Floors are swept and vacuumed before anything goes down so no grit gets trapped and scratches the finish.

  3. Install protection

    Protection board is rolled out, cut to fit tight to walls and cabinets, and seams are taped. Heavier panels go where equipment and materials will move.

  4. Maintain

    On longer projects we can come back to inspect, patch and replace worn sections.

  5. Remove

    At the end of the job we pull up all protection, dispose of it and leave the floors clean and undamaged.

How to Prepare

  • Clear rugs, furniture and items from the traffic paths
  • Let us know the flooring type, since some finishes need breathable protection
  • Tell us if new floors were recently finished, because they may need extra cure time
  • Share your contractor's schedule so protection is in place before day one

What Affects the Price

Floor protection is priced by square footage and the type of protection needed (breathable board, heavy-duty panels or carpet film), the number of stairs, and whether you want setup only or setup plus removal. It's a small fraction of the cost of repairing damaged floors.

FAQ

Remodeling Floor Protection FAQ

Will tape damage my hardwood finish?

We tape seams of the protection to itself, not to your floor, wherever possible. When tape must touch the floor, we use low-tack tape made for finished surfaces.

Can you protect newly installed floors?

Yes. New floors are often protected before other trades come in. Let us know the finish and how long ago it was applied.

Do you work for contractors?

Yes. Many contractors hire us to set up protection so their crew can start working right away.
