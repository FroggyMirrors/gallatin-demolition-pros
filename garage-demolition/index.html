Garage Interior Demolition

Professional Garage Interior Demolition in Gallatin

A lot of Gallatin garages have years of add-ons inside: old drywall and paneling, built-in shelving, cabinets, workbenches, pegboard walls, lofts and half-finished rooms. Before a garage conversion, new epoxy floor, storage system or fresh drywall, all of that has to come out.

We handle the inside of the garage only: walls, ceilings, built-ins and finishes, while the structure itself stays standing. We don't tear down whole garages, barns or other large buildings. If you need a full teardown, call us anyway and we'll point you to a trusted demolition contractor who handles that kind of job.

What's Included

  • Drywall, paneling and pegboard removal from walls and ceilings
  • Built-in shelving, cabinets, workbenches and lofts
  • Old insulation removal
  • Interior partition walls and half-finished rooms (non-load-bearing)
  • Old carpet, tile, rubber mats or vinyl over the slab
  • Protection of the garage door, opener and anything staying
  • Hauling all debris, with metal recycled when possible

What to Expect

How Your Garage Interior Demolition Project Works

  1. Free quote

    Send photos through the form or we stop by. We'll mark exactly what comes out and what stays, like the garage door, opener, water heater and electrical panel.

  2. Utilities

    Outlets and lights in walls being opened are de-energized, and any plumbing is capped by a licensed tradesperson before work starts.

  3. Protection

    We cover the garage door, opener, water heater and anything staying in place, and protect the path from the garage to our trailer.

  4. Tear-out

    Built-ins come out first, then wall and ceiling coverings and insulation, working in an orderly way so the space stays safe.

  5. Cleanup

    Everything is hauled away, and the slab is swept and magnet-swept for nails and screws so your next contractor can start right away.

How to Prepare

  • Empty the garage, or book a garage clean-out with us first
  • Mark anything that stays, such as shelving you want to keep
  • Move vehicles out for the day
  • Have your contractor confirm which walls, if any, are load-bearing

What Affects the Price

Garage interior demolition pricing depends on the size of the garage, how many walls and ceilings are being opened, how many built-ins there are, whether there's insulation, and how much debris it all makes. We give a firm, upfront price before we start.

FAQ

Garage Interior Demolition FAQ

Do you tear down whole garages or barns?

No. We focus on the inside of the garage. For a full garage, barn or large building teardown, call us and we'll refer you to a trusted contractor who handles structural demolition.

How long does garage interior demolition take?

Most one- or two-car garages are gutted in one day, depending on how many walls, ceilings and built-ins are coming out.

Can you clear out the garage first?

Yes. We can do a garage clean-out and the interior demolition in the same visit.
