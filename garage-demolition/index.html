Garage Demolition

Professional Garage Demolition in Gallatin

Detached garages, carports and pole barns are bigger projects than sheds. They usually have electrical service, sometimes a concrete slab and footings, and the roof and framing are heavier. Garage demolition takes planning, the right utility disconnects, and a crew that knows how to bring a structure down in a controlled way.

We demolish detached garages, carports, pole barns, workshops and the like across Gallatin and Sumner County. Attached garages are a different job, since they share a wall with your home, so we'll talk through what's possible during your quote.

What's Included

  • Site walk and demolition plan
  • Guidance on utility disconnects and permits
  • Removal of roofing, siding, doors and windows
  • Controlled tear-down of framing
  • Slab, footing and apron removal (optional)
  • Hauling all debris, with metal and concrete recycled when possible
  • Final grading rake-out and cleanup

What to Expect

How Your Garage Demolition Project Works

  1. On-site quote

    Garages usually get an in-person look. We check the size, roof type, utilities, slab, access and anything close to the structure such as trees, fences or your house.

  2. Permits and utilities

    Garage demolition in Gallatin often requires a demolition permit, and electrical, gas and water must be disconnected first. We walk you through what's needed.

  3. Strip-out

    Doors, windows, garage door tracks, wiring and any contents are removed first so materials can be separated for recycling.

  4. Structural demolition

    The roof and walls are taken down in a controlled sequence, away from your home, fences and trees.

  5. Slab and cleanup

    If you want the slab gone, it's broken up and hauled. The site is raked smooth and cleared of nails and debris.

How to Prepare

  • Empty the garage or tell us what goes and what stays
  • Schedule the power disconnect, and gas if it's present
  • Move vehicles and anything parked near the garage
  • Let neighbors know there will be equipment and noise for a day or two

What Affects the Price

Garage demolition pricing depends on square footage, the number of stories, roofing material, whether there's a slab and how thick it is, utility work, access for equipment, and how much is stored inside. We provide a written, itemized quote after the site visit.

FAQ

Garage Demolition FAQ

Do I need a permit to demolish a garage in Gallatin?

In most cases a detached garage with utilities needs a demolition permit through the City of Gallatin. Requirements in unincorporated Sumner County may differ. We'll help you sort it out.

How long does garage demolition take?

A typical one- or two-car detached garage is usually done in one to two days, plus slab removal if included.

Can you demolish an attached garage?

Sometimes. Because it shares a wall and roofline with the house, it needs careful engineering and usually a contractor to close up the house afterward. We'll discuss it during your quote.
