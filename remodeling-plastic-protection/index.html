Remodeling Plastic Protection

Professional Remodeling Plastic Protection in Gallatin

Construction dust is extremely fine. It travels through doorways, under doors and through your HVAC system, settling on furniture, electronics and every surface in the house, sometimes for weeks after a project ends. For families with allergies, asthma, kids or pets, containment matters even more.

We set up professional dust containment for remodels and demolition: tension-pole plastic walls, zipper entry doors, sealed HVAC vents and returns, and plastic sheeting over furniture and belongings that can't be moved. We work for homeowners and contractors, and can maintain and take down the containment when the project is complete.

What's Included

  • Floor-to-ceiling plastic barrier walls with spring-loaded poles
  • Self-adhesive zipper doors for entry into the work zone
  • Sealing HVAC supply vents and returns in the work area
  • Covering furniture, electronics and belongings with plastic sheeting
  • Sealing doorways to rooms not being worked on
  • Sticky mats at the zipper door to catch dust on shoes
  • Teardown and disposal at the end of the project

What to Expect

How Your Remodeling Plastic Protection Project Works

  1. Plan containment

    We walk the space and decide where barriers go, how workers will get in and out, and which vents and doorways must be sealed.

  2. Prep

    Furniture is moved or covered, and floor protection goes down along the traffic path.

  3. Install barriers

    Plastic walls are installed floor to ceiling with tension poles that won't damage ceilings or walls, and zipper doors are added for access.

  4. Seal HVAC

    Vents and returns in the work zone are sealed to keep dust out of your ductwork. We recommend upgrading or changing your air filter after the project.

  5. Maintain and remove

    Barriers are checked and repaired as needed. At the end, plastic is carefully folded inward to trap dust and removed.

How to Prepare

  • Remove or set aside valuables, electronics and fragile items
  • Let us know about family members with allergies or breathing issues
  • Plan to replace your HVAC filter after the project
  • Coordinate the setup date with your contractor so containment is up before demolition

What Affects the Price

Plastic protection is priced based on the number of barrier walls, ceiling height, zipper doors, vents to seal, and how much furniture or how many belongings need covering. Setup, maintenance visits and removal can be priced together or separately.

FAQ

Remodeling Plastic Protection FAQ

Will the poles damage my ceiling?

The tension poles use padded heads that hold the plastic tight without fasteners, so they won't damage ceilings or walls.

Is this only for demolition?

No. Containment is useful for drywall sanding, painting, flooring removal, cabinet refinishing and any dusty remodeling work.

Can you contain dust in an occupied home?

Yes. That's the main reason for containment. It lets your family live comfortably in the rest of the house during the project.
