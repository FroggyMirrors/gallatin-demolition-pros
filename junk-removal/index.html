Junk Removal

Professional Junk Removal in Gallatin

Whether it's one old couch, a garage full of stuff or the leftovers from a remodel, junk removal should be easy. You don't have to drag anything to the curb, rent a truck or make trips to the landfill. Our crew does all the lifting and loading from wherever the items are in your home or property.

We donate usable furniture and household goods to local charities whenever possible, recycle metal, electronics and cardboard, and only send to the landfill what can't be reused. Our goal is to keep as much as possible out of the Sumner County landfill.

What's Included

  • All lifting, carrying and loading from anywhere on your property
  • Furniture, mattresses and box springs
  • Appliances, electronics and exercise equipment
  • Yard waste, brush and storm debris
  • Construction and remodel debris
  • Hot tubs, playsets and trampolines
  • Sweeping up the area after items are removed

How Your Junk Removal Project Works

  1. Get a quote

    Use the form or call with a description or photos. We can give a price range up front and confirm it on site.

  2. Schedule

    Pick a time that works for you. Same-day and next-day pickups are often available in Gallatin.

  3. On-site price

    When we arrive, we look at everything and confirm the price before we lift a thing. There's no obligation.

  4. We load it up

    Our crew carries out everything you want gone, from the basement, attic, upstairs or backyard.

  5. Responsible disposal

    Items are sorted for donation, recycling and disposal. We sweep up before we leave.

How to Prepare

  • Make a list or mark the items that are going (sticky notes work great)
  • Set aside anything you're keeping so there's no confusion
  • Make sure we can park near the house or driveway
  • Remove hazardous items such as paint, chemicals and fuel, which we can't take

What Affects the Price

Junk removal is priced by how much space your items take up in our trailer, from a single item to a full load. Very heavy materials like concrete, dirt, shingles or tile are priced by weight. Labor, loading and disposal are all included in the price we quote.

FAQ

Junk Removal FAQ

What can't you take?

We can't haul hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals, solvents, fuel, motor oil, asbestos, medical waste or propane tanks. Sumner County holds household hazardous waste collection events for these.

Do I need to be home?

It's best if you're there to confirm the items and the price. For curbside or exterior pickups, we can often work with you by phone and photos.

Do you donate items?

Yes. Usable furniture and household goods go to local donation centers whenever possible.
