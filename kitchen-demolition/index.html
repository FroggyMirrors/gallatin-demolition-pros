Kitchen Demolition

Professional Kitchen Demolition in Gallatin

Kitchen demolition is more involved than it looks. There are plumbing and gas connections, heavy stone or laminate countertops, upper cabinets anchored to studs, tile backsplashes bonded to drywall, and often several layers of flooring. Taking it out carefully protects the rest of your home and gives your remodeler a clean start.

We can do a full gut down to the studs and subfloor, or a selective tear-out where we save the cabinets and remove only the counters and backsplash, for example. If you want to donate your old cabinets or appliances, we remove them carefully so they're still usable.

What's Included

  • Appliance disconnection (by a licensed pro where gas or hardwire is involved) and removal
  • Countertop removal: granite, quartz, laminate, tile and butcher block
  • Upper and lower cabinet removal, careful removal for reuse if requested
  • Backsplash tile and drywall removal
  • Flooring removal down to the subfloor
  • Soffit, bulkhead and pantry removal
  • Full haul-away and broom-clean finish

What to Expect

How Your Kitchen Demolition Project Works

  1. Plan the scope

    We confirm exactly what comes out, what stays, and whether anything is being saved or donated.

  2. Shut-offs

    Water lines are shut off and capped, and gas and hardwired appliances are disconnected by a licensed pro. We confirm everything is safe before starting.

  3. Protection

    We protect floors from the kitchen to the exit, hang plastic barriers to adjacent rooms and cover HVAC vents.

  4. Tear-out

    Appliances first, then countertops, upper cabinets, lower cabinets, backsplash, soffits and flooring, in that order, to keep the room safe.

  5. Haul and clean

    Debris is carried out as we work. The kitchen is left swept, vacuumed and ready for the next trade.

How to Prepare

  • Empty all cabinets, drawers and the pantry
  • Set up a temporary kitchen in another room
  • Unplug small appliances and remove items from counters
  • Coordinate the schedule with your remodeler, plumber and electrician

What Affects the Price

Kitchen demolition pricing depends on the kitchen size, number of cabinets, countertop material (stone is heavy and must be handled carefully), how much tile there is, flooring layers, whether items are being saved, and the distance to the exit. Most kitchen demolitions are completed in one to two days.

FAQ

Kitchen Demolition FAQ

Can you save my cabinets for donation?

Yes. If they're in good shape, we remove them carefully and can drop them off at a local donation center or leave them for you.

Do you disconnect the gas stove?

Gas lines should be disconnected and capped by a licensed plumber or gas technician. We remove the appliance once it's safely disconnected.

How long will my kitchen be out of commission?

Demolition itself usually takes one to two days. Your overall remodel timeline depends on your contractor.
