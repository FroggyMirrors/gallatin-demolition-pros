Home Surface Protection

Professional Home Surface Protection in Gallatin

In any remodel, there's more in the house that's staying than going. Granite countertops get chipped by dropped tools, door frames get gouged by ladders and materials, stair railings get scratched, stainless appliances get dented, and windows get splattered. Repairs after the fact are expensive and often never quite match.

Home surface protection covers everything that isn't being remodeled. We protect countertops, cabinets, door jambs, stair rails, banisters, appliances, windows, tubs and showers, fireplaces and fixtures using the right material for each surface. This service pairs with our floor protection, tape-off and plastic protection for complete coverage.

What's Included

  • Countertop protection with cushioned, self-adhesive or padded covers
  • Cabinet face and door protection
  • Door jamb and casing guards along work paths
  • Stair railing, banister and newel post wraps
  • Window, glass and mirror protection film
  • Appliance covers for stainless steel and glass surfaces
  • Tub, shower and fixture protection

What to Expect

How Your Home Surface Protection Project Works

  1. Walk-through

    We list every surface in and around the work area and along material paths that could be damaged.

  2. Choose materials

    Each surface gets the right protection: cushioned covers for stone, film for glass, guards for door frames and wraps for railings.

  3. Install

    Protection is installed snugly and secured without damaging finishes.

  4. Check during the project

    On longer projects we check and repair protection as other trades work.

  5. Remove and clean

    Everything is removed carefully at the end, and surfaces are wiped down.

How to Prepare

  • Clear items off countertops and shelves
  • Point out any delicate or high-value finishes
  • Tell us which rooms and paths contractors will use
  • Coordinate setup with your contractor's first day

What Affects the Price

Surface protection is priced by the number and types of surfaces, the size of the work area, and the length of the project. It's commonly bundled with floor protection and plastic containment at a combined price.

FAQ

Home Surface Protection FAQ

Is this only for big remodels?

No. Even a single-room project like a bathroom remodel puts countertops, door frames and railings at risk along the work path.

Will protection leave residue?

We use products designed for each finish and remove them on schedule so they don't leave residue or discolor surfaces.

Can you protect my home for movers or a flooring install?

Yes. Surface and floor protection is great for move-ins, move-outs, flooring installs and furniture deliveries.
