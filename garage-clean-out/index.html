Garage Clean-Out

Professional Garage Clean-Out in Gallatin

The garage is where everything ends up: boxes from the last move, old furniture, broken lawn equipment, leftover building materials, kids' bikes and sports gear. Before long the cars are parked in the driveway. A garage clean-out gets your space back in one visit.

You decide what stays. We help sort, carry everything out, load the trailer, and take items to donation, recycling or disposal. We can also remove old shelving, workbenches, cabinets and attic storage platforms if you're starting fresh.

What's Included

  • Help sorting keep, donate and toss piles
  • Removal of boxes, furniture and household items
  • Old lawn mowers, grills and bicycles
  • Shelving, workbenches and garage cabinets
  • Scrap metal, lumber and leftover building materials
  • Sweeping out the garage floor
  • Donation drop-off and recycling

What to Expect

How Your Garage Clean-Out Project Works

  1. Quote

    Send photos of the garage or schedule a visit. We'll give you a price based on volume.

  2. Sort

    On the day of the job we can help you sort through items. Anything you're keeping is set aside.

  3. Clear out

    Our crew carries out and loads everything that's going, including large items like freezers or workbenches.

  4. Sweep

    Once it's empty, we sweep the floor so you can see what you're working with.

  5. Donate and recycle

    Usable items go to donation, metal goes to recycling, and only the rest goes to the landfill.

How to Prepare

  • Go through boxes ahead of time if you want to save anything
  • Mark keep items or move them to one side
  • Remove paint, chemicals, fuel and propane, which we can't haul
  • Make sure we can back the trailer up to the garage door

What Affects the Price

Garage clean-outs are priced by volume, meaning how much of our trailer your items fill. A partial clean-out of a few large items costs less than a full two-car garage packed to the ceiling. Heavy items such as old concrete, tile or shingles may be priced by weight.

FAQ

Garage Clean-Out FAQ

How long does a garage clean-out take?

Most garage clean-outs take two to four hours, depending on how full the garage is and how much sorting is needed.

Can you take old paint cans?

We can take paint cans that are completely dry and empty. Liquid paint and chemicals must go to a household hazardous waste collection.

Do you also tear down old garages?

Yes. See our garage demolition service if the structure itself needs to go.
