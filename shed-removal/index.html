Shed Removal

Professional Shed Removal in Gallatin

Sheds are one of the most common jobs we do in Gallatin. Years of Tennessee humidity, termites and storms take a toll on wood sheds, and metal sheds rust through at the seams. Once a shed starts leaning, leaking or housing critters, removing it is usually cheaper and safer than repairing it.

We handle sheds of every type and size: small garden sheds, 10x12 and 12x16 storage buildings, lean-tos, kit sheds, metal sheds and larger barn-style outbuildings. If the shed is still full, we can empty it first and haul the contents in the same trip.

What's Included

  • Emptying the shed of stored items (if needed)
  • Disconnecting and capping any simple electrical runs by a licensed electrician when required
  • Tear-down of walls, roof, doors and windows
  • Removal of the wood floor, skids or runners
  • Breaking out a concrete slab or gravel pad (optional add-on)
  • Hauling every piece of debris to proper disposal or recycling
  • Raking the area and picking up nails with a magnetic sweeper

What to Expect

How Your Shed Removal Project Works

  1. Free quote

    Send a photo or two through the form, or we come out and look. We'll ask about size, what it's made of, what's still inside it and what sits underneath.

  2. Scheduling

    Most shed removals are scheduled within a few days. If there's power to the shed, it needs to be disconnected before we start, and we'll tell you if that's the case.

  3. Tear-down

    We remove the roofing first, then walls and framing, working top-down so nothing collapses unpredictably. Most standard sheds take two to four hours.

  4. Floor and base

    We pull up the floor, skids and blocks. If there's a concrete slab and you want it gone, we break it up and remove it too.

  5. Cleanup

    Everything is loaded and hauled away. We rake the area and run a magnetic sweeper to grab stray nails and screws so the yard is safe for kids, pets and mowers.

How to Prepare

  • Remove anything you want to keep from the shed, or tell us what to save
  • Let us know if the shed has electricity or water run to it
  • Clear a path from the shed to the driveway or street if possible
  • Let your HOA know if your neighborhood requires notice for exterior work

What Affects the Price

Shed removal pricing depends on the size of the shed, what it's built from, whether there's a concrete slab, how full it is, and how close we can park our trailer. A small, empty wood shed near the driveway costs far less than a large shed in the back corner of a fenced yard with a slab underneath. We give you a firm price before any work starts.

FAQ

Shed Removal FAQ

Do I need a permit to remove a shed in Gallatin?

Most small accessory buildings without utilities do not need a demolition permit, but rules change. If the shed has electrical service or is large, we'll help you check with the City of Gallatin Codes Department first.

Can you remove a shed that has already collapsed?

Yes. Collapsed and storm-damaged sheds are common jobs. We just sort the debris and haul it away.

Can you take the concrete slab too?

Yes. Slab removal is an add-on. Let us know and we'll include it in your quote.
