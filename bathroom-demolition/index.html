Bathroom Demolition

Professional Bathroom Demolition in Gallatin

Bathroom demolition is some of the toughest interior work there is. Cast iron tubs can weigh 300 pounds or more, tile is often set in a thick mortar bed, and bathrooms are tight spaces where the rest of your home is just a doorway away. It's also where hidden water damage and rot are most often found.

We remove everything from a simple vanity and toilet swap to a full gut: tubs (including cast iron), fiberglass and tile showers, shower pans, tile floors and walls, vanities, toilets, mirrors, medicine cabinets, exhaust fans, drywall and subfloor where needed.

What's Included

  • Water shut-off and capping of supply lines
  • Toilet, vanity, sink and mirror removal
  • Tub removal, including breaking up cast iron tubs on site when needed
  • Shower surround, tile walls and shower pan removal
  • Tile floor and mortar bed removal
  • Drywall, cement board and damaged subfloor removal
  • Haul-away and a clean, vacuumed room

What to Expect

How Your Bathroom Demolition Project Works

  1. Scope

    We confirm what comes out and look for signs of water damage. We'll tell you what we find once walls and floors are opened.

  2. Protection

    Floors from the bathroom to the exit are covered, the doorway is sealed with a zipper dust barrier and HVAC vents are covered.

  3. Fixtures out

    Water is shut off and lines capped. The toilet, vanity and accessories come out first, and the drain is plugged to block sewer gas and debris.

  4. Tub, shower and tile

    Tile and backer board are removed, then the tub or shower. Heavy cast iron tubs may be broken into pieces to get them out safely.

  5. Clean and ready

    Debris is hauled out, studs and subfloor are cleaned of nails and tile remnants, and any damage is pointed out to you and your contractor.

How to Prepare

  • Remove towels, toiletries, rugs and anything stored in the bathroom
  • Plan for the family to use another bathroom
  • Let us know where the main water shut-off is
  • Coordinate timing with your plumber and remodeler

What Affects the Price

Bathroom demolition pricing depends on the size of the bathroom, the type of tub (cast iron takes more labor), how much tile there is and whether it's on a mortar bed, number of fixtures, damaged subfloor, and how far it is to carry debris out of the house. Most bathrooms are done in a day.

FAQ

Bathroom Demolition FAQ

Can you remove a cast iron tub?

Yes. We either carry it out whole or break it into pieces in place, depending on the layout and what you prefer.

What if you find water damage or mold?

We'll stop and show you. Small areas of damaged subfloor or drywall can usually be removed as part of the job. Larger mold problems should be handled by a remediation specialist.

Can you remove just the tile and keep the tub?

Yes. Selective demolition is common, and we'll protect the tub while we work.
