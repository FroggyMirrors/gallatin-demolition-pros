Crawlspace Clean-Out

Professional Crawlspace Clean-Out in Gallatin

Many Gallatin homes are built over crawlspaces, and they collect a surprising amount of junk over the years: torn plastic vapor barrier, insulation that's fallen from the floor joists, scrap lumber left by builders, old ductwork, pipes and sometimes years of stored items. That debris holds moisture, attracts pests and hides problems.

A clean crawlspace is the first step before encapsulation, waterproofing, pest treatment, plumbing repairs or foundation work. We crawl in, remove everything that shouldn't be there, bag it, and haul it away so your contractor can see and do their job.

What's Included

  • Removal of old and torn vapor barrier
  • Removal of fallen or wet fiberglass insulation
  • Construction debris such as scrap wood, bricks and pipe
  • Abandoned ductwork and old materials
  • Stored items and junk
  • Bagging everything inside the crawlspace for clean carry-out
  • Haul-away and cleanup around the access point

What to Expect

How Your Crawlspace Clean-Out Project Works

  1. Inspection

    We look in the crawlspace to check access, height, moisture and how much debris there is. Photos from the access door help us quote.

  2. Safety check

    We check for standing water, exposed wiring and signs of animals before anyone goes in.

  3. Bag and remove

    Our crew wears protective suits and respirators, bags debris in place and drags it out to keep mess off your yard and home.

  4. Final pass

    We do a final pass to pick up remaining scraps and leave the dirt or gravel floor clear.

  5. Haul-away

    All bags and debris are hauled off. We'll share photos of anything concerning, such as leaks or damaged joists.

How to Prepare

  • Make sure the crawlspace access door or hatch is accessible
  • Let us know about any known leaks, standing water or pest activity
  • Tell us what's going in next, such as encapsulation or repairs, so we know what to remove
  • Keep pets away from the access area during the job

What Affects the Price

Crawlspace clean-outs are priced based on the square footage of the crawlspace, its height (low crawlspaces are slower), the amount and type of debris, moisture conditions and how far the access door is from our trailer.

FAQ

Crawlspace Clean-Out FAQ

Do you do crawlspace encapsulation?

No, we handle the clean-out so your encapsulation or waterproofing contractor starts with a clean, debris-free space.

Do you remove mold?

We remove debris and damaged materials, but mold remediation should be done by a certified specialist. We'll let you know if we see signs of it.

How low of a crawlspace can you work in?

We regularly work in crawlspaces 18 inches high and up. We'll let you know during the inspection if access is a problem.
