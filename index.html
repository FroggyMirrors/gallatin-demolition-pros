FAQ

Demolition & Junk Removal Questions

How much does demolition cost in Gallatin, TN?

Cost depends on the size of the structure, materials, access and disposal. Small jobs like a shed or deck cost far less than a garage or full interior tear-out. We give free, no-obligation quotes so you know the price before work begins.

Do I need a permit to demolish a structure in Gallatin?

Many demolition projects in Gallatin require a permit from the City of Gallatin Codes Department, and utilities must be disconnected first. We can walk you through what your project needs.

What junk do you haul away?

Furniture, appliances, mattresses, yard waste, construction debris, hot tubs, playsets, garage, attic and crawlspace clutter, and full estate clean-outs. We cannot take hazardous materials such as paint, chemicals or asbestos.

Do you protect my home during a remodel?

Yes. We offer floor protection, surface protection, professional tape-off and plastic dust barriers, either with our demolition work or on their own for your contractor's project.

How fast can you come out?

We are open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, and we are local to Gallatin, so we can often provide same-day or next-day quotes and junk pickups. Larger demolition jobs are scheduled around permits and utility disconnects.

Do you recycle or donate?

Yes. Usable items are donated when possible and metal, concrete and other materials are recycled to keep as much as possible out of the landfill.
