Remodeling Tape-Off

Professional Remodeling Tape-Off in Gallatin

Good tape-off is the difference between a clean finished project and hours of scraping, touch-ups and cleanup. Whether it's prep for painting, drywall finishing, texture, spraying or demolition, careful masking protects everything that isn't being worked on.

We provide tape-off and masking for homeowners, painters, drywall finishers, cabinet refinishers and general contractors. We use the right tape for each surface, from delicate low-tack tape on fresh paint and wallpaper to heavy-duty tape on exterior surfaces, and we remove it cleanly when the work is done.

What's Included

  • Masking trim, baseboards, crown molding and door casings
  • Covering windows, glass and mirrors with masking paper or film
  • Masking cabinets, countertops and built-ins
  • Covering outlets, switches, light fixtures and thermostats
  • Masking hardware, hinges and plumbing fixtures
  • Pre-taped masking film for walls and large surfaces
  • Clean tape removal at the right time to avoid peeling

What to Expect

How Your Remodeling Tape-Off Project Works

  1. Scope

    We confirm what's being painted, sprayed or worked on and exactly what needs protection.

  2. Surface check

    We check for fresh paint, wallpaper or delicate finishes and select the right tape for each surface.

  3. Masking

    Tape is applied in straight, sealed lines, and paper or film covers larger areas. Edges are pressed down to prevent bleed-through.

  4. Inspection

    We walk through with you or your contractor to make sure nothing was missed before work starts.

  5. Removal

    Tape is removed at the right time, usually while finishes are still slightly soft, at a low angle to keep lines crisp and avoid lifting paint.

How to Prepare

  • Remove wall hangings, curtains and decor
  • Move furniture to the center of the room or out of it
  • Let us know how recently any surfaces were painted
  • Share your painter's or contractor's start date so masking is done on time

What Affects the Price

Tape-off is priced by room or square footage and depends on the amount of trim, windows, cabinets and fixtures to mask, whether spraying is planned (which requires more coverage), and whether removal is included.

FAQ

Remodeling Tape-Off FAQ

Do you do the painting too?

No. We focus on prep and protection so your painter can work faster and cleaner.

Will the tape pull off my paint?

We choose tape based on the surface and how long it will stay on, and we remove it carefully to avoid lifting paint.

Can you mask for a sprayed ceiling or cabinet job?

Yes. Spray jobs need full coverage of everything nearby, and we'll mask accordingly.
