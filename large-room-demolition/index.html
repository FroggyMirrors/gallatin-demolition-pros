Large Room Demolition

Professional Large Room Demolition in Gallatin

Large rooms such as finished basements, bonus rooms over garages, great rooms, sunrooms and small office or retail suites generate a lot of debris quickly. Hundreds of square feet of drywall, flooring and ceiling material add up to multiple truckloads, and good planning keeps the job moving and the property clean.

We size our crew and haul-out plan to the space. For large jobs we may bring a dumpster or make multiple trailer runs. For homes, we focus on protecting the rest of the house, and for commercial spaces we work around your schedule, including after-hours if needed.

What's Included

  • Full gut-outs of basements, bonus rooms and great rooms
  • Drop ceiling grid and tile removal
  • Drywall, paneling and insulation removal
  • Carpet, tile, laminate and glued-down flooring removal
  • Interior partition and non-load-bearing wall removal
  • Light commercial tenant-improvement strip-outs
  • Dumpster coordination or multiple haul-outs as needed

What to Expect

How Your Large Room Demolition Project Works

  1. Site visit

    We measure the space, identify the materials, check access and stairs, and plan where debris will go.

  2. Containment

    The space is sealed off from the rest of the building with plastic barriers, and floors along the path out are protected.

  3. Utilities

    Electrical circuits in the demolition area are de-energized and plumbing is capped by licensed trades before walls come down.

  4. Demolition

    We work in zones from ceiling to floor so debris is managed and the space stays safe to walk.

  5. Final clean

    All debris is hauled, the space is swept and vacuumed, and you get a clean shell ready for construction.

How to Prepare

  • Empty the room of furniture, storage and belongings
  • Confirm the demolition scope and any walls with your contractor or engineer
  • For commercial spaces, coordinate building access and work hours with property management
  • Arrange for any required permits

What Affects the Price

Large room demolition is priced on square footage, materials and number of layers, ceiling type and height, stairs and access, haul-out volume (dumpster vs. trailer runs) and scheduling needs such as after-hours work. You get a detailed written quote after the site visit.

Large Room Demolition FAQ

Do you do basement demolition?

Yes. Basements are one of our most common large-room jobs, especially after water damage or before a full refinish.

Do you handle commercial interior demolition?

Yes. We do light commercial strip-outs for offices, retail and restaurant spaces in the Gallatin area.

Will I need a dumpster?

For many jobs we haul everything ourselves. For very large projects we may arrange a dumpster, and that's included in your quote.
