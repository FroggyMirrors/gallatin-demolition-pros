Deck Removal

Professional Deck Removal in Gallatin

A deck that's soft underfoot, has loose railings or is pulling away from the house isn't just ugly, it's a safety hazard. Many Gallatin homes have decks built 15 to 30 years ago that have reached the end of their life, and replacing them always starts with careful demolition.

We remove pressure-treated wood decks, composite decks, multi-level decks, porches, stairs, pergolas and railings. We take care around your siding, windows, doors and landscaping so the house is ready for your builder or new patio.

What's Included

  • Removal of decking boards, railings, balusters and stairs
  • Removal of joists, beams and posts
  • Removing the ledger board from the house without damaging the siding or sheathing
  • Pulling or cutting concrete post footings (as quoted)
  • Removal of attached pergolas, benches and planters
  • Hauling all wood, composite and metal debris
  • Magnetic nail sweep and full cleanup

What to Expect

How Your Deck Removal Project Works

  1. Free quote

    Tell us the approximate size, height and material, or send photos. Elevated and second-story decks take more time and safety planning.

  2. Protecting the house

    Before tear-down we cover nearby windows, doors and AC units and move outdoor furniture and grills out of the work area.

  3. Top-down tear-out

    We remove railings and boards first, then the joists, beams and posts in a controlled order so the structure never falls on its own.

  4. Ledger and footings

    The ledger board is carefully detached from the house. We leave the area ready for flashing or siding repair, and pull or cut footings as agreed.

  5. Haul-away and cleanup

    All debris is loaded and hauled away. We rake and magnet-sweep the area so no nails or screws are left behind.

How to Prepare

  • Move grills, furniture, planters and decorations off the deck
  • Unplug and remove any lights or electrical on the deck
  • Keep pets and kids inside during demolition
  • Tell us if you're having a new deck built so we can coordinate with your contractor

What Affects the Price

Deck removal pricing is based on square footage, height off the ground, number of levels and stairs, the material (composite is heavier), how many footings need to come out, and access to the backyard. We give you a clear price before we start.

FAQ

Deck Removal FAQ

How long does it take to remove a deck?

Most single-level decks under 300 square feet are removed in one day. Large or elevated decks can take a little longer.

Will you damage my siding?

We take care at the ledger board. Some siding or flashing is often worn behind an old deck, and we'll point out anything we see so your builder can address it.

Can you remove just the railings or stairs?

Yes. Partial removals for repairs or rebuilds are no problem.
