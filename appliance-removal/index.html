Appliance Removal

Professional Appliance Removal in Gallatin

Old appliances are heavy, awkward and hard to get rid of. Many can't just be set at the curb, and refrigerators, freezers and AC units contain refrigerants that must be handled properly. Our crew takes them out of your kitchen, laundry room, basement or garage without scratching your floors or walls.

Most appliance metal is recyclable. We take working appliances to donation or resale partners when possible and bring the rest to scrap metal and appliance recyclers so they don't end up in a landfill.

What's Included

  • Refrigerators, freezers and mini-fridges
  • Washers, dryers and laundry pedestals
  • Stoves, ranges, wall ovens, microwaves and dishwashers
  • Water heaters (once disconnected and drained)
  • Window AC units, dehumidifiers and outdoor condensers
  • Careful removal with floor and doorway protection
  • Recycling or donation of every appliance possible

What to Expect

How Your Appliance Removal Project Works

  1. Quote

    Tell us how many appliances and where they are in the house. Single-item pickups are welcome.

  2. Disconnection

    Appliances need to be unplugged, with water lines shut off and gas lines capped by a licensed pro. We'll tell you if anything needs to be done ahead of time.

  3. Protection

    We protect floors and door frames along the path out, and use dollies and straps for heavy items.

  4. Removal

    Appliances are carried out and loaded. Fridge and freezer doors are secured for transport.

  5. Recycling

    Working units are donated when possible, and the rest go to a certified recycler.

How to Prepare

  • Empty and defrost refrigerators and freezers a day ahead
  • Drain washers and water heaters, or let us know so we can plan
  • Have gas appliances disconnected and capped by a licensed pro
  • Clear a path from the appliance to the door

What Affects the Price

Appliance removal is priced per item, with discounts for multiple appliances. Location (basement or second floor), size, and whether it requires special refrigerant handling affect the price slightly.

FAQ

Appliance Removal FAQ

Do you take refrigerators with freon?

Yes. Refrigerant-containing appliances are taken to recyclers that properly recover the refrigerant.

Can you disconnect my gas dryer or stove?

Gas appliances must be disconnected and capped by a licensed professional. Once that's done, we'll remove them.

Do you remove water heaters?

Yes, once the water heater has been disconnected and drained.
