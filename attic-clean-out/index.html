Attic Clean-Out

Professional Attic Clean-Out in Gallatin

Attics are hard to work in. Narrow pull-down ladders, exposed joists you can't step between, no flooring, and summer temperatures well over 120 degrees in Middle Tennessee. That's why so many attics fill up with stuff that never comes back down.

We carry everything down safely and load it out. That includes stored boxes and furniture, old luggage, holiday decorations, construction debris left by builders, abandoned ductwork, and damaged batt insulation. We work early in the day during summer to avoid the worst heat, and we protect the floors and walls below the attic opening.

What's Included

  • Removing stored boxes, furniture and household items
  • Holiday decor, luggage and old electronics
  • Construction leftovers and debris from roofers or builders
  • Old ductwork and abandoned materials
  • Removal of loose or damaged batt insulation
  • Floor and wall protection below the attic access
  • Sorting for donation, recycling and disposal

What to Expect

How Your Attic Clean-Out Project Works

  1. Quote

    Photos of the attic (even a quick phone video from the ladder) help us estimate volume and access.

  2. Plan around heat

    In warm months we schedule early-morning starts to keep the crew safe and work efficient.

  3. Protect below

    We lay floor protection below the attic opening and along the path to the door.

  4. Carry down

    Items are passed down carefully, never dropped, and our crew walks only on joists or decking to protect your ceilings.

  5. Haul and sweep

    Everything is loaded and hauled away, and we clean up the area below the attic access.

How to Prepare

  • Tell us about anything up there you want to keep
  • Make sure the attic access ladder or stairs are in safe working order
  • Clear the hallway or closet below the attic opening
  • Let us know if you've seen signs of animals or pests

What Affects the Price

Attic clean-outs are priced by volume plus access. A walk-up attic with flooring is quicker than a pull-down ladder over a narrow hallway with no decking. Insulation removal is priced separately because of the extra time and protection involved.

FAQ

Attic Clean-Out FAQ

Can you remove blown-in insulation?

Loose blown-in insulation requires specialized vacuum equipment, and we can refer you to an insulation contractor. We remove batt insulation and debris.

Will you damage my ceiling?

Our crew only steps on joists or decking and moves carefully. We'll point out any areas that look unsafe before we start.

What if there are signs of animals?

Let us know ahead of time. Active animal infestations should be handled by a wildlife or pest professional first.
