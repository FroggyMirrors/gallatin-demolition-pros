Fence Removal

Professional Fence Removal in Gallatin

Fences take a beating in Middle Tennessee. Wood fences rot at the base and lean after a few wet seasons, and storms regularly knock down whole sections. Before a new fence can go in, or if you just want your yard opened up, the old fence has to come out completely.

We remove wood privacy fences, picket fences, chain-link, vinyl, split-rail, wire and farm fencing, and gates. The most important part is the posts. We pull them out with their concrete footings so your new fence installer has clean holes or a clean yard to work with.

What's Included

  • Removal of panels, pickets, rails and gates
  • Removal of chain-link fabric, top rails and tension wire
  • Pulling posts along with their concrete footings
  • Backfilling post holes with soil if requested
  • Hauling all wood, vinyl, metal and concrete
  • Recycling metal fencing whenever possible
  • Cleanup of nails, staples and debris

What to Expect

How Your Fence Removal Project Works

  1. Free quote

    Tell us the length of fence (a rough guess is fine), the material and the number of gates. A photo of a post base helps us know whether they're set in concrete.

  2. Utility check

    Pulling posts means digging. If we're going deeper than the old footings, we make sure Tennessee 811 has marked any buried lines first.

  3. Panel removal

    We remove panels, pickets and fabric section by section, keeping debris stacked so your yard stays usable during the job.

  4. Post extraction

    Posts are pulled with their concrete footings using a post puller or by digging them out. Broken posts are dug out, not just snapped off at ground level.

  5. Haul-away

    All materials are hauled off. Holes are backfilled if you want, and we clean up the fence line.

How to Prepare

  • Talk with your neighbor if the fence is on a shared property line
  • Keep pets inside since the yard will be open during removal
  • Move plants, decor or items stored along the fence
  • Tell us about any sprinkler lines or invisible dog fence near the fence line

What Affects the Price

Fence removal is usually priced by the linear foot, with adjustments for the material, fence height, number of gates, whether posts are set in concrete, and how easy it is to get debris to the trailer. Chain-link usually costs less than wood privacy fence because it's lighter and the metal can be recycled.

FAQ

Fence Removal FAQ

Do you remove the concrete around the posts?

Yes. We pull posts with the concrete footing attached whenever possible, so nothing is left buried in your yard.

Can you remove part of a fence?

Yes. We do partial removals for storm damage or to make room for a gate, driveway or addition.

Do you install new fences?

No, we focus on removal. We leave the fence line clean and ready for your fence installer.
